The Story

It started with algún día.

While Chris and Tatiana were dating, they traded "someday" daydreams. After they married, someday became a daily ritual: a chocolate-note coffee with fresh-ground cinnamon bark and panela — the same cup, every morning, for more than eighteen months.

This blend recreates that exact cup, with beans grown near Tatiana's Colombian hometown. Three people, independently and without planning it, reached for the same word to describe it. So that's what it's called.

Two stories. One cup.
